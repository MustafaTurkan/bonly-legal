## Frequently asked questions

### How do I restore my Pro purchase?

Open the Pro screen in the App and use the restore purchases option while signed in to the same Apple ID or Google account you
used to buy Pro. If you linked Apple or Google sign-in to Bonly, use the same sign-in on your new device so your data comes back as well.

### How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

Pro is billed by Apple or Google, so you manage and cancel it in your store account:

- **iPhone / iPad:** Settings → your name → Subscriptions, or
  [apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions)
- **Android:** Google Play → Payments & subscriptions, or
  [play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions)

Cancel at least 24 hours before the end of the current period to avoid the next renewal. Deleting your Bonly account does
**not** cancel the subscription. Refunds are handled by Apple / Google under their policies. See
[Terms of Use §6]({{ '/terms/' | relative_url }}#subscription).

### How do I export my data?

In the App: Settings → Export. You can download a CSV file, or all your data and optional receipt images as a ZIP (the download
link is valid for 24 hours). CSV export is always free.

### How do I delete my account?

In the App: Settings → Delete account and data. If you can no longer use the App, you can ask us by e-mail. Full details,
including what is deleted and what is kept for a limited time: [Delete your account]({{ '/delete-account/' | relative_url }}).

### How do I turn off AI processing or usage data sharing?

In the App: Settings → Privacy. Both are optional and off unless you turn them on. See the
[Privacy Policy]({{ '/privacy/' | relative_url }}).
