# Delete your Bonly account

This page explains how to delete your account in **Bonly: Receipt & Budget** (developer: Mustafa Türkan) and its data.

## Option 1 — In the App (immediate)

1. Open Bonly.
2. Go to **Settings → Delete account and data**.
3. Confirm. Deletion is immediate and cannot be undone.

Before deleting, you may want to export your data: Settings → Export (CSV, or all data and optional receipt images as a ZIP).

## Option 2 — By e-mail (if you cannot use the App)

If you have deleted the App or can no longer access your account, send an e-mail to
[mustafatrkn93@gmail.com](mailto:mustafatrkn93@gmail.com?subject=Account%20deletion%20request) with the subject
**"Account deletion request"**. To find and verify your account we will ask you for information that identifies it (for
example, the e-mail address of the Apple or Google account you linked to Bonly). We complete the request within 30 days.

## Your subscription

Deleting your account does **not** cancel a Pro subscription. Cancel it in your store account:
[App Store](https://apps.apple.com/account/subscriptions) ·
[Google Play](https://play.google.com/store/account/subscriptions).

## What is deleted

Your account and all data stored in it: expenses and other records, budgets, goals, savings jars, recurring payments,
accounts / cards labels and balances, income and pay-day settings, names you entered for shared bills and IOUs, receipt
text and receipt images.

## What is kept, and for how long

| Data | Kept for |
| --- | --- |
| Backups | Up to 30 days; deleted accounts leave backups after this period |
| Account-deletion marker (user ID only) | 7 days, to complete the deletion |
| Server logs (IP address, user agent, user ID) | 30 days |
| Server request logs (duplicate-request protection) | 7 days |
| Subscription notification records | 30 days |
| Exported PDF files (server copy) | 1 day |
| Usage analytics / crash data (only if you enabled sharing) | Analytics 14 months; Crashlytics 90 days |
| Copy of an input sent for AI parsing at the AI provider (only with your consent) | Limited period under the provider's terms |
| Purchase records held by Apple / Google | Under Apple's / Google's own policies |
